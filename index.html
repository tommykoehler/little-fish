linger longer

Little Fish is a bakery and natural wine bar inspired by the warmth and simplicity of French boulangeries and natural wines. Created as a space for community and conversation, it invites guests to slow down, connect, and linger a little longer.

Rooted in the spirit of one glass of wine turning into a three-hour conversation around shared tables, Little Fish is built on the beauty of breaking bread and the belief that there is something sacred in gathering. It is a place where hospitality is intentional, connection is prioritized, and everyone has a seat at the table.
